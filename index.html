Before you dive

There's nothing to win here

Lumen Tide is meant to help you relax. There's no score, no timer and no way to lose.

Every creature you touch plays a note. The notes are made fresh as you go, so the song you create has never been heard before and won't be heard again.

Each touch also lays a few grains of sand into a mandala. Like the sand mandalas made by Tibetan Buddhist monks, it is built with care and then swept away with the tide.

So don't aim for anything or try to finish anything. Stay with whatever is drifting past right now, and let each note and each grain go as it fades.

Take a slow breath when you're ready.

Lumen Tide

Trace the dark water with light. Touch whatever drifts past and let it go.

Every pop adds a note and a few grains of coloured sand. Together they make a song and a sand mandala that will never come out the same way twice, and both drift away when you're done.

The longer you play, the deeper you drift, and new creatures come to meet you. Sound on; headphones are nice.
